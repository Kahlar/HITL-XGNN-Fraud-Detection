1. Tabular vs Graph Model Superiority:
   - Fact Checked: The report explicitly acknowledges that XGBoost achieves higher
     overall F1 (0.7223) and PR-AUC (0.6712) than GraphSAGE (F1 = 0.5175, Val PR-AUC = 0.7966)

   - Design Rationale: Rather than exaggerating GNN performance, the report details
     the operational complementary roles: XGBoost provides superior local transaction
     filtering, while GraphSAGE provides topological structural propagation essential
     for uncovering multi-hop money laundering patterns that tabular models miss.

2. TrainOnlyScaler Leakage Isolation Protocol:
   - Fact Checked: Table 3.1 rigorously documents that feature normalization parameters
     (mean μ_train and standard deviation σ_train) were computed strictly on labeled nodes
     in training timesteps t ∈ [1, 34]. No validation (t ∈ [35, 39]) or quarantine test
     nodes (t ∈ [40, 49]) were exposed during scaling.

   - Fact Checked: Section 4.9 explicitly clarifies that EXP-07 offline active learning
     benchmarks utilize a ground-truth simulation oracle with an audit feedback buffer.
     The study explicitly avoids claiming live human subject study results, and outlines
     future human subject IRB trials in Chapter 5.3.

   - Fact Checked: At a 20% feedback budget (1,097 reviewed transactions), uncertainty
     sampling achieves F1 = 0.5030 compared to random sampling F1 = 0.4401 (+6.29%

   - Fact Checked: GNNExplainer achieves Mean Fidelity⁺ = 0.1470 and Mean Fidelity⁻ = 0.0069
